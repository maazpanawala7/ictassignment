1. What is a computer network? Purpose in simple words:
A computer network is two or more devices (like PCs, phones, servers, or printers) connected together by cables or Wi-Fi. Its purpose is to let devices talk to each other, share files, and share things like internet access and printers.

2. Three types of networks (by area):
- LAN: Covers a small area like one room, house, or office with fast speeds.
- MAN: Covers a city or big campus by connecting LANs together.
- WAN: Covers huge areas like countries or continents (the Internet is a WAN).
