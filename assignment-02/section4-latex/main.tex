\begin{abstract}
This short paper looks at the red panda (\textit{Ailurus fulgens}), where it lives in the eastern Himalayas and nearby mountains, what it eats, and how it behaves in the wild.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The red panda is a small mammal found in cool mountain forests with bamboo. Even though its name sounds like a giant panda, it is in its own separate family and has reddish-brown fur and a long striped tail.

\section{Diet and Daily Habits}
Most of a red panda's diet is bamboo leaves and shoots, though it also eats fruit, berries, eggs, and insects when available. They spend a lot of time in trees and are mostly active around dawn, dusk, and night.

\section{Conservation}
Red pandas face threats from habitat loss, forest fragmentation, and illegal hunting. Saving connected forest areas and bamboo habitats is important to keep their numbers from dropping further.
